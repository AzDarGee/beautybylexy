  • Aquarius

  • January 20th – February 18th

  • Air

    Water Bearer

  • A planner and a creator, a lover and an intellectual. Terrifying and electric, tempting and unpredictable. Although he experiences life fully, he must learn to anchor his sense of self. In effort to seek self-understanding, an Aquarius will revolutionize for social progression. Aquarius seeks equality in all life forms and is on the pursuit to unify his soul with the greater universe. He is not distracted by other people’s behaviors or opinions but does desire to have intellectual companionship. Although an Aquarius appears to be soft and innocent, beware of his persistence in truth seeking. He will become impersonal and highly authoritative if necessary. It is this relentless pursuit of finding meaning in life that truly makes Aquarius wise.

  • Pisces

  • February 19th – March 20th

  • Water

    Fish

  • Mysterious and ethereal, she swims in all directions at once, searching for unknown realms in which only she may dwell. Due to her soul’s dual nature, she is an enigmatic creature of the ocean. Loyal yet detached, divine nothingness and infinite everythingness. As limitless as the cosmos themselves, defining her is an impossible task. Motivated to explore unfound inner territory, she swims deeper and deeper in the ocean of consciousness, encountering endless opportunities to rediscover all that already lies within. However, she is her own greatest obstacle to overcome in unearthing all of what she is by subjecting herself to self-imposed restraint. She often doubts her own immensity and holds too strongly onto the past. She must let go of self-criticism and extreme sensitivity to truly be happy. Her unconditional acceptance and openness to others leaves her vulnerable to the ill intention of others. When she is upset, she retreats from the world and actively sheds all the negative energy that she absorbs from her environment. Highly intuitive and complex, she desires something beyond the physical and lives in a dream-like state, continuously searching for other worlds to eventually unify her soul with the universe at large.

    “Study me as much as you like. You will not know me. For I differ in a hundred ways from what you see me to be.” - Rumi

  • Aries

  • March 21st – April 19th

  • Fire

    Ram

  • Though symbolized by the ram and known as the warrior sign, Aries quietly accesses higher consciousness with diligent focus and discipline. It is essential for an Aries to explore the self in order to activate the soul and body connection. With this alignment between the self and human experience, an Aries is an unstoppable leader. Encompassing the element of fire, an Aries spirit is expressed with electric qualities. With a well-established self, Aries is independent and strong, fearlessly accepting any challenge that comes their way. Unfortunately, their relentless persistence may come with as aspect of stubbornness and an inflexible approach to achieving their goals.  

    If an Aries can separate dominance from aggression, they have the power to make those around them feel protected. Allow an Aries to freely express who they are because this warrior must also accept their own sensitivity to resist acting on impulse. You can expect Aries to relentlessly lead with its two horns at an unwavering speed and focus while simultaneously embracing togetherness.

  • Taurus

  • April 20th – May 20th

  • Earth

    Bull

  • Known as the bull, Taurus is an incredible force that is easily angered when provoked. However, this is due to her earthly element that makes her not only sensitive but highly receptive to her environment. Consequently, she is driven by physical sensation and practicality. She is easily drawn to love, beauty and all physical pleasures rendering her a highly sensual being. Due to her incredible observant qualities, Taurus soaks up her environment like a sponge and is able to store any relevant information for later use. This helps her to be strong, incredibly self-reliant, and consistent. However, she is impatient and demands to be in charge, even when it is inappropriate. Although they are not abstract creatures, they search for beauty in their immediate environment and approach the world in a realistic and factual sense.

  • Gemini

  • May 21st – June 20th

  • Air

    Twins

  • Full of wisdom, Gemini unifies the mind and heart to influence experiences of reality. Driven by intellect and curiosity, a Gemini is adaptable to her environment and seeks social interaction. Gemini is a dual sign and therefore changes in personality occur frequently. A Gemini can experience love and hate at the same time and often struggles to understand personal feelings. Because of their fluid and shapeless nature, it’s no wonder that Gemini encompasses the element of air. Never fixed and always mutable, these twins may appear to be creative and energetic one day and then superficial and irresolute the next. Despite a Gemini’s constant change in persona, they posses the ability to analyze situations from all angles, giving them an enormous amount of insight.

  • Cancer

  • June 21st – July 22nd

  • Water

    Crab

  • Extremely intuitive and sentimental, Cancer will go above and beyond to help others but will likely withhold her own feelings from others to avoid conflict. However, Cancer must let go of being possessive over those she cares for deeply. Cancer is motivated by self-preservation, subjecting her to easily become attached to not only people but also material things. If cancer overcomes her fear of separateness, she will move away from being overly reactive and protected of her relationships. Only then will her concept of self emerge. Her limitless compassion and nurturing energy to all life forms will finally include self-love. She will learn that her possessiveness and extreme jealousy make her appear to be selfish although she is truly loyal, caring and dependable.

  • Leo

  • July 23rd – August 22nd

  • Fire

    Lion

  • Confident and devoted, you can expect to hear a Leo say “I will” and they will. In constant search for wholeness and individuality, a Leo must learn self-mastery to achieve success. However, their need for love, acceptance and dominion often leads them on a self-destructive path, motivated by the wrongful desire for an external throne. A Leo’s need to dominate acts as a barrier to his own creative nature that would truly allow him to make a lasting impact on his goals. His excessive need for attention will motivate him to acquire material possessions in order to show off as this temporarily raise his self-esteem. However, if this lion can turn inwards and expand his awareness, he will let go of his overbearing ego and the need to be the “single-creator”. He will now view himself as a “co-creator” and will understand the significance of assimilating multiple personalities together in order to lead. This newfound urge towards self-mastery will allow him to become a member of a group in which he can still inspire. Although a Leo can be lazy, he is ambitious, optimistic and the ultimate entertainer when he is happy. He is magnetic and romantic, illuminating his ability to channel powerful energy from within out into the world.

  • Virgo

  • August 23rd – September 22nd

  • Earth

    The Virgin

  • Rigid, orderly and rule abiding, Virgo craves control, organization and purification. Virgo is not concentrated on how other perceives her in order to focus her energy on rule of order. Bound to perfectionism, Virgo is analytical to the point of self-destruction. Virgo is the perfect resource in practical settings that require hard word but her exceptionally high standards damage her relationship not only with herself but also with those who she cares about. Although she may be perceived as judgmental, she simply cannot help her unique ability to spot imperfection. Virgo is the sign of giving and will go the extra mile to produce the highest standard of work.

  • Libra

  • September 23rd – October 22nd

  • Air

    The Scales

  • Motivated by the need to find balance, Libra continuously strives to perfect her judgment in order to balance all polarities of life – good and bad, male and female energies. Libra has a strong desire to find a soul mate but must learn to balance will, love and the intellect. Libra, the ultimately a warrior, must quiet the ego to truly make meaningful connections. By riding the ego, Libra is powerful in creating unity between all things. Not only is Libra highly extroverted but also spiritually beautiful, seeking love in all things. She perceives the world as a unified whole that is intricately connected.

  • Scorpio

  • October 23rd – November 21st

  • Water

    Scorpion

  • With a penetrating stare, and an intense demeanor, this goddess perfects the dance of temptation. Although she is divine, beware as she is poisonous. Motivated by desire, she strategically uses her sensuality to persuade others for personal gain. However, Scorpio is a loyal creature, especially to her family. She is ambitious, assertive and courageous. You can expect a Scorpio to stand strongly by your side. Her perceptual talents allow her to be sensitive to the needs of others and she will willingly sacrifice herself for those who remain truthful to her. She dislikes passivity and secrets and therefore it may be hard to gain her respect.

  • Sagittarius

  • November 22nd – December 21st

  • Fire

    Half Human / Half Animal

  • This mutable sign passionately searches for wisdom to unfold a higher goal or ultimate purpose in life. However, Sagittarius procrastinates by focusing on emotional desires, which are never truly satisfied. He irresponsibly uses his mind to justify what his body craves and this prevents him from rightfully directing his energy into a consciously intended goal. If he can learn to control his animal nature (the horse), he can direct this passion to achieve ultimate success. When trusting his intuition, he can learn to resolve his duality and achieve balance between the body and soul. This will allow him to learn how to listen to others and perceive the world as is truly is.

  • Capricorn

  • December 22nd – January 21st

  • Earth

    Mountain Goat

  • Capricorn is a prisoner to material form, allowing little freedom for her soul to explore life forms that are not visible to the naked eye. Unfortunately, she is driven to achieve physical goals that never lead to any real satisfaction. As she is a sensitive sign, she resists looking inwards during conflict to protect herself from admitting to any personal imperfections. Unfortunately, this makes her powerless to rigid ways of thinking and she must learn to expand her mind. If she can awaken the spirit within, she can utilize her internal urge to create material forms with purpose. Although ambitious, she must learn to detach from the outcome from her actions, and focus on the ever present now. Only then she will find happiness in the process of her creations.
