  • About Me

  • Alexandra (Lexy) Delalis

  • I always envisioned myself choosing a path that energized ambition out of pure excitement. For me, makeup is an art form that is empowering, healing and fun. While in University, I found myself missing the freedom to create art and explore the world on my own terms. One day, I remember doing my makeup and feeling not only inspired, but also completely lost in the art. As such, I daydreamed about creating my own makeup business. I realized the therapeutic value of rediscovering yourself with complete acceptance and having the courage to express it to the world unapologetically. So, upon graduation, I decided to chase this dream relentlessly by tuning into the most primitive core of my being. Because makeup artistry is inherently driven, you can expect originality and the highest standard of professionalism and cleanliness. I take pride in my work and I love to continuously build my skills and try new things. Getting your makeup done is a form of self-care and there is nothing more rewarding to me then people leaving my service feeling both beautiful and confident.
